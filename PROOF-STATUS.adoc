|===
|Checked result |Evidence
|Ordinary evidence-refined fibre encoding
|`Core.from-to` and `Core.to-from`: both pointwise inverse laws.
|Inhabited cases; claims quantify over every candidate
|`Core.Case`, `Core.Holds`; claims are not limited to the chosen inhabitant.
|Conditional actual-world soundness
|`Core.actual-world-sound`: observation equality and evidence for the actual
world remain required arguments.
|Presence is unresolved without the bound
|`Presence.residual-alone-does-not-imply-presence` and
`Presence.residual-alone-does-not-imply-absence`, using `(0,2)` and `(2,0)`.
|Bounded noise establishes presence
|`Presence.bounded-presence`: for natural numbers, `u+n=2` and `n≤1` imply `u≠0`.
|Bounded noise warrants presence for the actual world
|`Presence.bounded-warrants-actual-presence`: `observe actual ≡ 2` and

|Presence does not identify the value
|`Presence.bounded-value-not-identified`: `(1,1)` and `(2,0)` disagree on `u`.

|Inconsistent constraints cannot supply a case
|`Presence.conflict-has-no-candidate` and `Presence.conflict-has-no-case`:
adding `u=0` to the bounded case is contradictory.
|Identification is possible with stronger evidence
|`Presence.no-noise-identifies-two`: adding `n=0` identifies `u=2`.

|Evidence refinement transports a conditional claim
|`Core.refine-claim`: requires an inhabited new case and an implication from
the new evidence to the old evidence. It does not invent consistency.
|===

=== Contexts of assumptions

Evidence is a context `Ctx W = List (W → Set)`, interpreted as the product of
its assumptions, with order-preserving thinnings `Δ ⊆ Γ` between contexts.

[cols="2,3",options="header"]
|===
|Checked result |Evidence
|Evidence restricts along a thinning
|`Context.weaken`: a thinning `Δ ⊆ Γ` maps evidence for `Γ` to evidence for `Δ`.
|Cases and claims move in the right directions
|`Context.case-weaken` (a case under more assumptions is a case under fewer) and
`Context.claim-strengthen` (a claim under fewer assumptions holds under more,
via `Core.refine-claim`).
|Nothing thins into the empty context from nothing
|`Context.no-thinning-into-empty`: a non-empty context has no thinning into `[]`.
|===

Source: link:src/ResidualEvidence/Context.agda[Context].

=== Dependency-preserving composition

[cols="2,3",options="header"]
|===
|Checked result |Evidence
|Concatenated evidence splits and rejoins exactly
|`Composition.join-split` and `Composition.split-join`: both inverse laws for
`Γ ++ Δ`; `split-is-weaken-left` and `split-is-weaken-right` identify the
projections with thinnings.
|Claims compose without inventing consistency
|`Composition.compose-claims`: the composed case is an explicit argument, never
derived from the two components; `component-left` and `component-right`
recover each component case.
|Paired observations are an ordinary fibre
|`Composition.pair-from-to` and `Composition.pair-to-from`: both inverse laws
between candidates of a paired observation and pairs of candidates.
|Coarsening transfers claims in one direction only
|`Composition.coarsen-candidate`, `coarsen-case` and `coarsen-claim`: a coarse
claim transfers to the fine observation, not the other way.
|Worked example
|`Examples.Composition.present-and-two`: `[NoiseBound] ++ [NoNoise]` yields
presence and `u ≡ 2`; `coarsening-forgets-presence`: under the coarse
"positive or not" observation the silent candidate `(0,1)` is admitted.
|===

Sources: link:src/ResidualEvidence/Composition.agda[Composition] and
link:src/ResidualEvidence/Examples/Composition.agda[Composition example].

=== Evidence revision and retraction

Refutation and disagreement are constructive witnesses, not negations of
`Holds`, so they survive `--safe` and transport along retractions.

[cols="2,3",options="header"]
|===
|Checked result |Evidence
|Constructive refutation implies the negative
|`Revision.refuted-not-holds` and `Revision.disagreeing-not-identified`.
|Retraction is a thinning with its own inverse laws
|`Revision.retract-⊆`, `retract-join-split`, `retract-split-join` and
`retract-is-weaken`.
|What survives a retraction
|`Revision.retract-case`, `refuted-retract`, `disagreeing-retract`;
`survives-retraction` for a claim whose support is kept, and
`dependent-holds` / `survives-dependent` for claims that rest on named assumptions.
|Revision replaces an assumption
|`Revision.replace-head` and `Revision.revise`: the revised case is an explicit
argument.
|Worked example
|`Examples.Revision.presence-survives` retracting `NoNoise`, while
`identification-lost` and `identification-not-recovered` show the value is no
longer identified; `presence-refuted` after retracting `NoiseBound`, via the
witness `(0,2)`; `balanced-identifies-one` after revision; `no-revised-case`
when the revised assumptions are inconsistent.
|===

Sources: link:src/ResidualEvidence/Revision.agda[Revision] and
link:src/ResidualEvidence/Examples/Revision.agda[Revision example].

=== Certified finite checker

The checker works over the explorer's own model: signed integers `u, n` in
−6..6, a noise bound 0..6, the three observation views (exact, sign,
magnitude) and the optional assumption that the latent is zero, giving 546
configurations. `Checker.Evidence cfg` is a three-assumption context, so the
in-range condition the explorer hides inside its loop bounds is an explicit
premise here.

[cols="2,3",options="header"]
|===
|Checked result |Evidence
|Integer arithmetic and the range
|`Finite.Int.range-sound` and `range-complete`: `range` is exactly −6..6.
|Admissibility is decided correctly
|`Finite.Soundness.admits-sound` and `admits-complete`.
|Enumeration is exactly the admissible in-range worlds
|`Finite.Soundness.enumerate-sound` and `enumerate-complete`.
|Every presence verdict is sound
|`entailed-sound`, `refuted-sound`, `unresolved-sound` and
`inconsistent-sound`; `entailed-complete` for the converse of entailment.
|Identification verdicts are sound
|`identified-sound`, `unidentified-sound` and `unidentified-refutes`.
|A finite verdict reaches an actual world only under all three assumptions
|`Finite.Soundness.finite-actual-sound`: observation equality, the in-range
condition, the noise bound and (if assumed) the zero latent are all explicit
premises, discharged through `Core.actual-world-sound`.
|===

=== Explorer correspondence

`just correspondence` runs `scripts/correspondence.sh`, separately from the
fast `just check`:

. `bun test tests/correspondence`: the harness loads the model from the
  unchanged `residual-evidence-explorer.html` and checks its identity, the
  order of the 546 configurations, hand-derived known-answer rows and the
  shape of the emitted table.
  The script asserts exactly 11 passing tests and none failing, because
  `bun test` exits 0 when it finds no tests.
. `generate-table.js` regenerates `ExplorerTable.agda` (546 rows) and `diff -u`
  requires it to equal the committed table byte for byte.
. `Correspondence.checker-matches-explorer : table ≡ expected` is proved by
  `refl`: the certified checker and the explorer agree on all 546
  configurations, row by row.

This certifies the checker against the explorer. It does not relate the
explorer to the first milestone's `Core` example, which uses unbounded natural
numbers where the explorer uses bounded signed integers: they remain
different models.

== Expected rejections

`just check` first type-checks the positive modules, then requires every
module under `tests/reject` to fail at its intended declaration:

* `FalsePresence`: using the bounded theorem after dropping its evidence.
* `FalseIdentification`: claiming every bounded candidate has `u=2`.
* `InconsistentCase`: constructing a case whose witness violates the noise bound.
* `FalseWeakening`: reading a thinning backwards, which would manufacture a
  noise bound out of no assumption.
* `FalseCoarsening`: transferring a fine claim to the coarse observation, which
  also admits the silent `(0,1)`.
* `FalseRetraction`: keeping presence after retracting `NoiseBound`, the
  assumption it rests on.
* `FalseIntEquality`: deciding that −1 and 1 are equal.
* `FalseFiniteEntailment`: claiming entailment where the checker refutes
  presence (residual 2, bound 6, exact view, zero latent assumed).
* `FalseFiniteSoundness`: transferring a finite verdict to an actual world
  without the in-range premise.

The runner checks the diagnostic shape as well as the exit status. A missing
module, parse error, unresolved name or unresolved hole does not count as a
successful expected rejection. Diagnostic logs go to ignored `_build/check-logs/`.
