= Residual Evidence Types: first research milestone
:icons: font

*Status: work in progress, 2026-09-09.* The imported assessment and explorer
are the starting material. The separate Agda starter archive mentioned in
the assessment is not included; its reported proofs have not been reproduced here.

== Start with presence without identification

Use the assessment's natural-number example `r = u + n = 2`.

. Define `Candidate observe r E` as a world paired with observation equality
  and evidence that the world satisfies `E`.
. Make an inhabited case explicit, so an inconsistent set of assumptions
  cannot become a useful warrant for every claim.
. Define claims over every admissible candidate, and identification of a
  query as agreement on its value across those candidates.
. Exhibit `(0,2)` and `(2,0)` without a noise bound: the residual alone
  does not establish presence of `u`.
. Add `n ≤ 1`: prove `u` is nonzero, while `(1,1)` and `(2,0)` still prevent
  identifying a unique value.
. Keep the premise that the actual world belongs to the candidate set
  separate from these model-relative results.

Recover the original starter first if it is available. Otherwise label the
new implementation and its checks as new work. An Agda module is only a
checked result after the declared prover and options accept it; the explorer
is only certified after a correspondence with its signed finite model is proved.

== Compare before expanding

The candidate object is an evidence-refined observation fibre. Compare its
encoding against Echo Types and its claim obligations against Epistemic
Types before introducing a new calculus. Presence, value identification,
source identity and causal role remain distinct questions.

Next, investigate dependency-preserving composition, revision and retraction
of evidence, and correspondence with the finite explorer. A certified checker
must account for the explorer's signed domain, bounds, retained observation
and inconsistent cases. Candidate counts are not probabilities.

== Ownership and shared meanings

The residual definitions, examples, explorer, tests and proofs belong in this
repository. The cross-project map, shared glossary and connection obligations
belong in https://github.com/hyperpolymath/nextgen-typing[nextgen-typing].
Echo's fibre/retention interfaces belong in `echo-types`; standpoint access,
warrants and sound proof transport belong in `epistemic-types`.

Confounding types remain a prospective causal specialisation. A causal model
and identification assumptions must be supplied before that term becomes a
formal interface or a claim about a hidden contribution.
