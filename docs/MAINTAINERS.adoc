== Responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for:

* reviewing and merging pull requests;
* triaging issues and research questions;
* keeping the proof record (`PROOF-STATUS.adoc`) accurate, with a hosted run
  cited for every verification claim;
* refreshing the pinned sibling revisions used by the integration checks;
* responding to security reports.

== Contribution process

. Open an issue describing the change and what it should prove or fix.
. Submit a pull request; the core checks, the expected rejections and the
  sibling comparisons must pass in CI.
. The maintainer reviews and merges.

== Decision making

With a single maintainer, decisions are made by that maintainer and recorded
in the issue or pull request that carries them; see
link:GOVERNANCE.adoc[GOVERNANCE.adoc] for the rules that bind those decisions.

== Becoming a maintainer

Contributors who show sustained, careful work on the proofs and the record may
be invited to join the roster at the maintainer's discretion.
