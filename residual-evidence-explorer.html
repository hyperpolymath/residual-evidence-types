Residual Evidence

Something may contribute to an observation without being identifiable from it. Explore exactly what follows from a residual, a noise bound, and the distinctions you retain.

Declare the model

r = u + n

r: residual · u: unresolved contribution · n: noise contribution. This additive model is an explicit assumption.

A chosen bound restricts this model. It is not evidence that real noise obeys it.

The complete model domain is integer u and n from −6 to 6. No probabilities, independence, causal roles, or real-world measurements are supplied.

What can you conclude?

Why this matters

“Present” asks whether every compatible world has a nonzero u. “Identified” asks whether they all agree on its value. Neither tells you the identity or causal role of a hidden source.

Try identifying u, then retaining only the residual’s absolute magnitude: the sign becomes unavailable. Try removing the noise bound: a previously justified presence claim may become unresolved. An empty set means the assumptions conflict; it does not make every claim true.

These results are conditional on the declared finite model. Counting candidate worlds does not assign probabilities. The Agda example uses natural numbers; this signed example is separately tested JavaScript, with no proved extraction correspondence.
